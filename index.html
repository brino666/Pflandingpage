How it works

Thais learns quietly in the background and just gets better the more you use it.

  • 1

    You work. Thais learns.

    Drop in your thoughts, notes, or rough ideas. There's no wrong way to start. Thais observes patterns in what you do — not what you say.

  • 2

    Memory that earns its place.

    Thais only remembers what will still be useful. Preferences, goals, and workflow patterns are quietly distilled — raw conversations are never stored.

  • 3

    It offers. You decide.

    When Thais notices a pattern worth keeping, it asks once: "I can remember that if you like." Say no and it never asks again. Say yes and it saves silently — you're always in control.

  • 4

    Encrypted. Yours alone.

    Everything is encrypted with a key only you hold. Nothing here is disclosed anywhere, with anyone, without your knowledge. Not even us.
